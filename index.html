The product and traffic were both strong, but customers hesitated right before checkout. The bottleneck wasn't the ads — it was the product page and buying experience. I migrated the store to Shopify and rebuilt the product page as a proper funnel on the default checkout, with no third-party plugins.

What changed

Reviews and ratings moved to the top of the page, pricing was shown clearly (old price, current price, direct discount), shipping and quality objections were addressed in a confirmation section, and a simplified size guide by weight cut down on hesitation.

Structure

A mix of CBO + Minimum Spend Limit campaigns for scaling, and Cost Per Result (CBO) campaigns for aggressive high-volume days, with 2–3 creative angles per product (direct, emotional/storytelling, UGC) and near-hourly monitoring under a simple rule: pump, punish, or kill.

The goal from day one: high-volume sales, held at a low cost, with the best possible ROAS sustained over three months — not a short-term spike.

Process

Product selection weighed shipping ease, quality, and competitive pricing. I studied the market leaders and built messaging around four angles — price, quality, emotion, trust — and defined the ideal customer's needs, fears, and way of thinking so ad copy spoke their language, not the product's spec sheet.

Structure & scaling

Parallel CBO campaigns (300 EGP) and cost-cap campaigns (400 EGP, caps at 45/50/55 EGP) repeated across three broad ad sets with dynamic creatives, scaled gradually via budget doubling, with hourly breakdown checks and a "punishment" rule (cut budget) whenever cost per result drifted out of range — campaigns usually self-corrected.

Final insight

No hack or magic setting made the difference — it came down to product selection, competitive angles, broad targeting, creative discipline, and continuous monitoring.
